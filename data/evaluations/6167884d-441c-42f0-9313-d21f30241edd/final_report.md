# Evaluation Results

**Prompt:** what are the attached projects about


## Answer

 The provided excerpts list several projects funded under the Horizon 2020 program, which aims to strengthen the EU's scientific and technological foundations, create a European Research Area, and drive the EU towards a knowledge society and competitive economy. Here are the projects mentioned:

1. **Sustainable forestry** (ID: 862605) - This project falls under the SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Food security, sustainable agriculture and forestry, marine, maritime and inland water research, and the bioeconomy program.

2. **Sustainable Obesity Phenotypes to Optimize Future Therapy** (ID: 875534) - This project is also part of the SOCIETAL CHALLENGES program, specifically the Health, demographic change and well-being category.

3. **Innovative Medicines Initiative 2 (IMI2)** (ID: 875534) - This project is a collaboration aimed at working with vulnerable women to identify the best implementation gains by screening cervical cancer more effectively in European countries. It is also part of the SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Health, demographic change and well-being program.

4. **Treating and managing disease** - The details of this project are not explicitly provided, but it is part of the SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Health, demographic change and well-being program.

5. **Diagnosis and Management of Febrile Illness using RNA** (ID: 848196) - This project is focused on personalized molecular signature diagnosis for febrile illnesses and is part of the SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Health, demographic change and well-being, Health care provision and integrated care program.

The last project, "Diagnosis and Management of Febrile Illness using RNA," is further detailed in the document `CORDIS_project_848196_en.pdf`. It involves partnerships with 22 hospitals in 11 EU countries and biotechnology groups in academia, SMEs, and industry. The project is funded under the H2020-EU.3.1.6. - Health care provision and integrated care program.
